About the game

Lucky Games is a cheerful match-3 adventure designed for quick sessions and satisfying progression. Every eliminated candy earns the same base score, while smart four- and five-candy matches unlock power bonuses and the spectacular Sugar Storm.

Quick tips

Tap two neighboring candies to swap them. Match at least three of the same design. Four- and five-candy matches award extra power, and a full power meter triggers a Sugar Storm. Your unlocked levels and best star ratings are saved automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Why did my swap move back?

A swap is only accepted when it creates a match of three or more candies. Other swaps return to their original positions and do not use a move.

How do I clear ice tiles?

Create a match on an ice-covered position. Frozen Peaks levels are completed only after both the target score and ice objective are cleared.

How does the Inferno timer work?

Every successful move reduces the timer. Include a star candy in a match to restore it before it reaches zero.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Level unlocks and best star ratings are stored on your device. Removing the app may remove this local progress.
